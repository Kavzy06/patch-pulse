How this works

Patch Pulse pulls the newest English reviews from the Google Play Store for the country you pick, along with the app version each reviewer was running. Versions are grouped at whatever depth gives the most comparable releases for that app (Instagram 449, Duolingo 6.95, WhatsApp 2.26.38). If an app doesn't report versions, reviews are grouped by week instead.

Each review is split into sentences, and a sentence counts toward a category when it uses that category's vocabulary, so "love the app but the ads are unbearable" counts against ads without dragging down design. Each mention gets a sentiment score that blends the sentence's wording (VADER, extended with app terms like "crashing" and "laggy") with the review's star rating.

Releases with fewer than 2% of the pulled reviews are hidden, and cells with fewer than 8 mentions are left blank. Keyword matching can miss complaints phrased in unusual ways, and Hinglish or other mixed-language reviews are scored less reliably.
